Our Story

InderLal Constructions was founded by Vivek Gupta with a singular purpose: to help clients realise their dreams through top-quality construction and absolute integrity.

Over the years we have built a legacy of trust and excellence in North India – in education, healthcare, residential, commercial and institutional developments. Our philosophy is centred around one core belief: goodwill is our greatest asset.

As such, we are proud to say that 99% of our previous clients return to us for their next project – a testament to the relationships we build alongside structures.
